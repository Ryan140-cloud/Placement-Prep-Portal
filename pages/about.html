About Us

Built for students heading into placement season

Placement Prep Portal brings every stage of campus recruitment preparation onto one screen.

Our Mission

To give every student — regardless of college or coaching access — a structured, self-paced way to prepare for aptitude, coding and interview rounds before recruiters arrive on campus.

Our Vision

A future where placement preparation isn't scattered across a dozen bookmarked links, but organized into one clear path a student can follow from day one of their final year.

Why Placement Preparation Matters

Campus drives compress years of learning into a few rounds: an aptitude test, a coding round, and interviews — all within a single day. Without focused practice beforehand, even strong students can lose out simply from being unfamiliar with the format, timing, or type of questions asked. Structured preparation closes that gap.

What Students Can Learn

  • Topic-wise resources for aptitude, reasoning and verbal ability
  • Coding and DSA practice organized by language and concept
  • Technical and HR interview preparation guides
  • Company-wise preparation notes for common recruiters
  • Placement-style mock tests to simulate real drives
  • A simple account system to track your own progress
