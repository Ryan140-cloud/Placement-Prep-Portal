Using Placement Prep Portal

Please read these simple terms before using the site.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

A free educational project

Placement Prep Portal is a free, non-commercial college project built by Group 3G2 at Chitkara University. It is made for learning and practice. There are no fees and nothing to buy.

We try to keep questions, tips and company details correct, but they may contain mistakes, and hiring patterns change every year. Always check important details on official sources.

Links to other websites

Many links (resources, YouTube, books, company careers pages, job listings, GitHub) go to third-party websites. We don't control them, and their own terms and privacy policies apply when you visit them.

Job listings

Jobs are fetched automatically from Himalayas and are not checked or verified by us. Research the employer before applying, and never pay anyone to get a job or an interview.

No guarantee of placement

Using this site does not guarantee a job, an interview or any result. Mock test scores and the readiness score are practice guides only, not predictions.

Your account

Accounts are a demo and are saved only in your own browser (see the Privacy Policy). Don't use a real password, and remember that clearing your browser data deletes your account.

We may update these terms as the project changes. The date at the top shows the latest version.
