Privacy Policy

Your data stays in your browser

Placement Prep Portal is a student project with no server database. This page explains, in simple words, what is saved and where it goes.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

What is saved, and where

Everything is saved only in your own browser, using a feature called localStorage. We cannot see it, and it never reaches a database of ours, because we don't have one. It includes:

  • Your account: name, email, phone, college, course, graduation year and password
  • Who is signed in right now, and your email if you tick “Remember Me”
  • Your mock test history and how many resources you explored
  • A short-lived copy of your GitHub profile card
  • Your light / dark theme choice
  • A backup copy of messages you send through the Contact form

Because this is a demo, passwords are stored as plain text in your browser. Please don't reuse a password you use anywhere else.

Services we send data to

A few features need another website to work. Only the data each one needs is sent:

  • Contact form: your name, email, phone, subject and message are emailed to the project team through Web3Forms.
  • Jobs page: your search words are used to fetch listings from Himalayas.
  • GitHub card on the Dashboard: the GitHub username you enter is looked up on the public GitHub API.
  • Coding Basics test and Tip of the Day: questions and tips come from Open Trivia DB and the Advice Slip API. Nothing about you is sent.
  • Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts.

How to delete your data

Log out, then clear this website's data in your browser settings (often under Privacy → Site data or Cookies and site data). That removes your account, history and settings from this browser for good.

Messages already sent through the Contact form are in the project team's email inbox. Ask us to delete them using the address in the footer.
